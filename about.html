Who We Are

A Peaceful Retreat Between Mountains & Forests

Menzah Bellota is an eco-lodge nestled in the heart of northern Morocco, just 15 minutes from Ouezzane and 45 minutes from Chefchaouen.

Surrounded by cork oak, myrtle, lavender, rosemary, and ancestral farmland, our location invites guests to reconnect with untouched nature in the Rif region. We offer a peaceful retreat between mountains, forests, and authentic rural landscapes that have shaped life in this corner of Morocco for generations.

We are strategically positioned near the Brikcha Ecological Reserve, a protected SIBE (Site d'Intérêt Biologique et Écologique) and an essential part of the Mediterranean Intercontinental Biosphere Reserve (MIBR). This unique setting makes Menzah Bellota an ideal destination for eco-tourism, biodiversity discovery, and sustainable travel experiences.

🌿 Protected Ecosystem

Brikcha Ecological Reserve
A Treasure of the Rif

Menzah Bellota is located near the Brikcha Ecological Reserve, one of the most important preserved ecosystems in northern Morocco. Closer to Ouezzane than Chefchaouen, this reserve is part of the Mediterranean Intercontinental Biosphere Reserve (MIBR) and holds official recognition as a SIBE (Site d'Intérêt Biologique et Écologique).

The reserve features centuries-old cork oak forests, diverse wildlife, medicinal plants, and landscapes shaped by traditional agricultural practices. Our eco-lodge actively collaborates with local stakeholders and environmental initiatives to promote sustainable land management, biodiversity conservation, and responsible tourism.

How We Got Here

Our Journey

From a dream in the mountains to a recognized model of sustainable tourism in northern Morocco.

Chapter 01

Foundation of Menzah Bellota

Abdelghani and Siham establish Menzah Bellota as a peaceful eco-lodge where travelers can reconnect with nature and explore northern Morocco's authentic culture.

Chapter 02

Expansion into Eco-Tourism

Menzah Bellota introduces guided hikes, forest walks, organic farming experiences, and partnerships with local artisans to promote sustainable rural tourism.

Chapter 03

Creation of MENZAH IZAREN Cooperative

The cooperative brings together local youth to develop cultural tourism, preserve heritage, and create responsible economic opportunities for the community.

Chapter 04

International Recognition

Menzah Bellota becomes recognized as a model for sustainable tourism in northern Morocco, attracting eco-conscious travelers from around the world.
